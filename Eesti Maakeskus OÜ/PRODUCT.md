# PRODUCT.md — Eesti Maakeskus

register: brand

## Product purpose

A single-page, mobile-first lead-generation site for **Eesti Maakeskus** (operated by
**L.T Estate OÜ**), a land-acquisition and real-estate value-creation company in Estonia.
The page exists to turn a landowner's visit into one of two actions: submit the inquiry
form, or open WhatsApp. A visitor must understand within five seconds: we buy land across
Estonia, we make a fast offer, we respond within 24 hours, and selling is simple.

## Users

Estonian landowners who want to sell. Often in a specific life situation: inherited land
they don't want to manage, a need for fast cash, a mortgage or debt tied to the property,
a move abroad, a plot sitting unused, a property that hasn't sold through normal channels,
or a "problematic" parcel with messy documents. They are not real-estate professionals.
They want clarity, speed, and trust, not jargon.

## Brand

- **Name:** Eesti Maakeskus · **Company:** L.T Estate OÜ · **Domain:** maakeskus.ee
- **Slogan:** *Anname maale uue väärtuse.* ("We give land new value.")
- **What we are:** a land buyer, a real-estate value-creator, a solver of problem
  properties, a development partner. Not a brokerage, not a flipper, not a speculator.
- **Promise:** to landowners — make selling simple, fast, trustworthy.
- **Manifesto line:** "We see opportunity where others see problems."

## Tone / voice

Clear, simple, concrete, professional, friendly, human. No heavy real-estate
terminology. If a landowner can't understand a sentence, rewrite it. Calm and confident,
never loud. Estonian language throughout.

## Brand personality

Calm · honest · entrepreneurial · trustworthy · smart · solution-focused · ambitious · modern.

## Strategic principles

- Conversion is the page's job: the form and WhatsApp must always be one tap away.
- Trust over hype. Specific, plain promises (24h response) beat superlatives.
- Image-rich: real Estonian land and landscape carry the emotional weight.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI real-estate template (navy + gold, three identical icon cards, stock "team").
- Loud, salesy, exclamation-heavy copy.
- Jargon-dense legalese.
